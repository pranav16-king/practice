🤖 LunaBot: Autonomous Navigation of Robot for Lunar Habitats

A ROS-based robotic prototype to autonomously navigate, map, and monitor future lunar habitats ensuring astronaut safety and operational reliability.

Background

With upcoming lunar missions aiming for sustained human presence, autonomous robotic systems are vital for habitat safety and operational reliability. The Moon’s absence of GPS, constrained spaces, and extreme conditions demand robots capable of independent navigation, mapping, and maintenance tasks to reduce astronaut workload. ROS (Robot Operating System) offers a modular framework ideal for such applications.

Detailed Description

Core Capabilities

  • Navigate indoor and outdoor lunar habitat environments.
  • Map and localize using LiDAR, cameras, and IMU sensor fusion.
  • Detect obstacles and hazards for safe path planning.
  • Monitor habitat parameters such as temperature and O₂ levels.
  • Perform routine patrols and alert signaling for maintenance.

Development Requirements

Participants will create robust ROS packages for navigation, mapping, and maintenance monitoring. All functionalities will be validated in a simulated lunar habitat environment.

Expected Solution

  • ROS package(s) implementing autonomous navigation, mapping, and maintenance monitoring.
  • Simulation demonstrating navigation and anomaly detection in a modeled lunar habitat.
  • A demo video showcasing the robot performing these tasks in the simulated environment.
